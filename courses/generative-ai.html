Course Overview

This 4-week Generative AI program is designed for builders who want practical, production-focused skills. You will start with LLM foundations, move into multimodal generation, then learn fine-tuning and RAG systems, and finish by deploying secure, observable, and cost-efficient GenAI applications.

Every week includes hands-on labs so you build real artifacts that can go directly into your portfolio.
