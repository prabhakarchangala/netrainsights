What You'll Learn

Explore the complete landscape of generative AI — from foundational models to building production-grade generative applications. Learn how GPT, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, and other models work, and how to fine-tune and deploy them.

  • LLM Architecture: Transformer internals, attention, tokenization
  • Prompt Engineering: Zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, and system prompts
  • Fine-Tuning: LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT — customize models on your data
  • Image Generation: Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 3, ControlNet
  • RAG Systems: Retrieval-augmented generation with vector stores
  • Deployment: APIs, Gradio, Streamlit, Hugging Face Spaces
