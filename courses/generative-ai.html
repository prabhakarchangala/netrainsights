Course Overview

Explore the frontier of generative AI. Learn to build models that create images, text, music, and more. Master GANs, VAEs, diffusion models, and modern text-to-image systems like DALL-E and Stable Diffusion.

  • GAN Variants (WGAN, StyleGAN, etc.)
  • Mode Collapse & Training Stability
  • Diffusion Models

  • Score-based Diffusion
  • Stable Diffusion Architecture
  • Text-to-Image Generation
  • Image Inpainting & Super-resolution
  • Conditioning & Control
  • Evaluation Metrics (FID, IS, Precision/Recall)
  • Real-world Applications
