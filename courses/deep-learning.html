Course Overview

Master deep learning from the ground up. This course covers neural network fundamentals, convolutional neural networks (CNNs), recurrent neural networks (RNNs), attention mechanisms, and transformer architectures that power modern AI systems.

  • Build neural networks from scratch
  • Understand backpropagation and gradient descent
  • Implement CNNs for computer vision tasks
  • Work with RNNs and LSTMs for sequential data
  • Master attention mechanisms and transformers
  • Train models on large datasets efficiently
  • Deploy deep learning models to production
