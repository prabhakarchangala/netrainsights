💬 Prompt Engineering

Master the art and science of communicating with AI models to get reliable, high-quality outputs

Level

Self-paced

What You'll Learn

Unlock the full potential of large language models through systematic prompt design. Learn how to craft prompts that produce consistent, accurate, and useful outputs across diverse AI applications.

  • Prompting Basics: Zero-shot, one-shot, few-shot patterns
  • Chain-of-Thought: Reasoning and step-by-step prompting
  • System Prompts: Role assignment and persona engineering
  • Output Control: Format constraints, JSON mode, structured output
  • Advanced Patterns: Self-consistency, ReAct, ToT (Tree of Thought)
  • Evaluation: Benchmarking and testing prompts systematically
