Course Overview

Learn to process, analyze, and generate natural language using state-of-the-art NLP techniques and models. From tokenization to transformers, master the tools used in modern language AI applications.

  • Text preprocessing & tokenization
  • Word embeddings & semantic similarity
  • Sentiment analysis & text classification
  • Named Entity Recognition & NER
  • Machine translation & seq2seq models
  • Question answering systems
  • Large Language Models & LLMs
